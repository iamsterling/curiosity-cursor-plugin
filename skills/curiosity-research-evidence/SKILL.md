# Research evidence

For the researcher only. Use for external or version-sensitive evidence; built-in Explore handles repository facts.

Label each material claim exactly `FACT|VENDOR_CLAIM|ACADEMIC_FINDING|INFERENCE|UNKNOWN`. Record direct origin, access date, applicable version, population, and scope. Preserve contradiction and negative result evidence. Require triangulation for consequential claims or state the single-source limitation. Give each question a decision verdict: supported, falsified, unresolved, or not applicable.

Curiosity is bounded: rank probes by decision value and cost, execute only the best authorized probe, and stop at coverage, saturation, exhaustion, or block. Saturation means further credible sources are unlikely to alter the decision; never imply certainty from search volume.
