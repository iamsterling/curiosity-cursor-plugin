# Decision design

For the strategist only. Establish the decision frame, constraints, and invariants. Present 2–4 viable options where credible; do not manufacture alternatives. Maintain an assumption and reversibility register. Run the owner-decision sweep across public API/config, data/persistence/migration/retention, dependency/license/supply chain, security/privacy/trust, deployment/operations, compatibility/rollout, paid service/spend, and reversibility/rollback.

Use measurable quality scenarios where evidence permits. Compare trade-offs, failure modes, validation cost, rollback, and second-order effects. Name the falsifier or reversal trigger. Recommend with rationale and identify the explicit owner decision and ADR need. Avoid routine implementation and speculative ceremony.
